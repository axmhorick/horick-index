Players come from each team's latest Ourlads depth chart (injured starters are replaced by their backups); ⭐ = on the 2027 NFL big board (⭐⭐ = top 50). Stars give their side a small edge on the plays they're part of. Teams without a depth chart yet show positions instead of names; charts are added each Thursday for that week's games.

🔊 Sound: crowd cheers, whistles and band riffs are generated in your browser; the crowd is the home crowd, so it cheers home-team plays and groans at the visitors' big ones. After a touchdown (and on 3rd down when you're on defense), tap ▶️ in the pop-up Spotify player to hear the fight song or stadium anthem (phones don't let sites start music on their own); logged out you'll hear Spotify's preview clip. Tap 🔊 on the scoreboard to mute.

How it works: each play's result is drawn from a distribution set by the two teams' offense and defense ratings and their real rushing and passing numbers. Your call matters: loading the box shuts down runs but opens up passes, blitzes get sacks but give up big plays, a two-deep shell takes away the deep ball. 4th-down and kicking decisions are yours on offense. Simplified college overtime (one possession each from the 25).
