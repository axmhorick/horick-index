How it works: each play's result is drawn from a distribution set by the two teams' offense and defense ratings and their real rushing and passing numbers. Your call matters: loading the box shuts down runs but opens up passes, blitzes get sacks but give up big plays, a two-deep shell takes away the deep ball. 4th-down and kicking decisions are yours on offense.
