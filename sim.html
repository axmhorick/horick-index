Players come from each team's latest Ourlads depth chart (injured starters are replaced by their backups); ⭐ = on the 2027 NFL big board (⭐⭐ = top 50). Stars give their side a small edge on the plays they're part of. Teams without a depth chart yet show positions instead of names; charts are added each Thursday for that week's games.

🔊 Sound: crowd cheers, whistles and band riffs are generated in your browser; the crowd is the home crowd, so it cheers home-team plays and groans at the visitors' big ones. After a touchdown (and on 3rd down when you're on defense), tap ▶️ in the pop-up Spotify player to hear the fight song or stadium anthem; it plays until your next call, then the drumline takes back over (phones don't let sites start music on their own); logged out you'll hear Spotify's preview clip. Tap 🔊 on the scoreboard to mute.

How it works: each play's result is drawn from a distribution set by the two teams' offense and defense ratings and their real rushing and passing numbers. Each offense plays to its identity from its real season numbers: option teams run on almost every down and take play-action shots, power teams pound it inside, Air Raid teams live on quick throws and screens and play fast, vertical teams push it downfield. Defenses adjust to who they're facing, and play action works best for teams that really run the ball. Defenses also adjust to what you keep calling: lean on one play too much and they sit on it. Your call matters: loading the box shuts down runs but opens up passes, blitzes get sacks but give up big plays, a two-deep shell takes away the deep ball. 4th-down and kicking decisions are yours on offense. Simplified college overtime (one possession each from the 25).
