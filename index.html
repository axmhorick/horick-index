How these rankings work
  • What it answers: who's best right now. Quality (offense − defense + special teams, in points) counts 55%.
  • Résumé (30%): each game's margin compared with what an average top-5 team would do against the same opponent, minus 7 points per loss. A close road loss to a great team barely hurts; losing to a bad team hurts a lot.
  • Schedule (15%): how hard each schedule has been, measured by the chance an average top-5 team would win those games. This counts on top of the schedule already built into the Résumé.
  • Margin: diminishing returns. Winning by 35 beats winning by 21, but not by much, and the Résumé caps margin at 50 points.
  • Recency: new games move Quality week by week and count 1.2x in the Résumé.
  • Extras: +0.75 points per true road win over an FBS team. Head-to-head: if two neighboring teams are within 1.5 points, the one that won their game ranks higher. My adj is your weekly gut call. Efficiency stats get added once a Game on Paper export is loaded.
  • Starting point: Week 11 Quality, Résumé and schedule numbers come from ESPN's SP+ (Nov. 8, 2026). From there, your system updates them every week. No conference labels: Group of 5 teams rise or fall on schedule and results alone.
